The protocol-v2 tests exercise relation, type, origin, non-stream Begin/Commit,
StreamStart, streamed chunks, StreamStop, StreamCommit, and StreamAbort. They
assert no delivery/checkpoint/acknowledgement before StreamCommit or after
StreamAbort, reject a streamed frame whose XID differs from the active stream,
and record the exact delivery order `emit -> receipt -> checkpoint -> ack`.
The recovery test verifies a receipt-less private transaction produces the typed
invalid-checkpoint rebootstrap outcome; the version test rejects PostgreSQL 13.
