# Discussion log — issue 4336

The launch brief settles the product decisions. The importer is neither a
provider repair tool nor a permissive parser: legitimate standard constructs
need correct generic support; invalid provider contracts retain their affected
operation through source-traced missing-foundation evidence; finite safeguards
are raised only after measurement and never removed.

The merged #4327 precedent is binding: no flattening, truncation, silent drop,
or connector-name branch. The external provider documents are evidence for the
shared importer, not files to edit.
